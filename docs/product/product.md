# ORION Product Record

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

ORION serves principals, institutional administrators, heads of department, class representatives, students, facilities and specialist staff, verified transport riders, and authorized club coordinators. Each user works only inside a current, server-authorized institution and role context.

## Product Purpose

ORION coordinates campus incidents from authorized intake through accountable human verification. Success means that a newly submitted incident can trigger a real model decision, a permitted action, durable follow-up, evidence review, and a materially changed recovery plan when work fails.

## Positioning

ORION is not a complaint inbox or a collection of role dashboards. Four bounded Featherless-powered modules share persistent operational state: Triage classifies untrusted reports, Commander creates and revises plans, Specialist selects currently eligible staff, and Verification evaluates structured evidence while required human checks remain authoritative.

## Operating Context

- A principal creates and governs a college context; administrators configure departments, rooms, roster rows, role grants, handlers, and two term-scoped CR seats.
- Authorized reporters submit routine, private, safety, transport, or club issues under distinct privacy and eligibility rules.
- Staff availability is independent from membership activity. Off-duty or inactive staff cannot receive ordinary work.
- Incidents move through versioned plans, assignments, evidence, approvals, notifications, human verification, reopening, replanning, or accountable escalation.
- Durable database jobs continue when browsers are closed. Email delivery state is separate from staff acknowledgement.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Next.js App Router, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Supabase Postgres/Auth/private Storage, Featherless, Resend, Vitest, Playwright, and Vercel.
- Tenant isolation, current membership checks, role scope, confidential case access, and explicit human approval are server-enforced.
- Report text is untrusted data and cannot grant permissions or expand tool access.
- Photos are private human evidence; the configured starting model is text-only.
- Staff submission alone never resolves physical or sensitive work.
- Dangerous physical, emergency, access-control, and sensitive-case decisions remain human controlled.
- P0 behavior is defined by the final handoff ZIP dated 4 September 2026. P1 and P2 items may be present only when honestly implemented and verified.

## Product Principles

1. Accountability over spectacle: every action, owner, version, and verification state remains explicit.
2. Authorization is live server state, never visual role-play or client metadata.
3. Automation remains bounded, durable, inspectable, and recoverable.
4. Sensitive information appears only to the smallest authorized audience.
5. The interface shows operational truth, including pending, failed, and deferred states.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Core workflows must work on mobile and by keyboard, expose clear focus, loading, empty, error, forbidden, and reduced-motion states, and maintain readable contrast. Safety communication must never imply that AI replaces approved emergency contacts.
